# How to Use the Symptom Chat Feature

## 🩺 Describing Symptoms in the Chat

Once you complete the medical consent form, you can describe your symptoms in natural language. The AI will extract and analyze them automatically.

## ✅ Example Messages That Work Well:

### Spanish Examples:
```
"Tengo dolor de cabeza intenso y fiebre alta"
"Me duele la garganta y tengo tos seca"
"Siento náuseas y dolor abdominal desde esta mañana"
"Me siento muy cansado y tengo escalofríos"
"Dolor muscular y dificultad para respirar"
```

### English Examples:
```
"I have a severe headache and high fever"
"Sore throat and dry cough for 2 days"
"Nausea and stomach pain since morning"
"Feeling exhausted with chills"
"Muscle pain and shortness of breath"
```

### Mixed Language:
```
"Tengo headache y fever desde ayer"
"Dolor de cabeza and fatigue"
```

## 🎯 Supported Symptoms:

### Pain Symptoms:
- Headache / Dolor de cabeza / Cefalea / Migraña
- Sore throat / Dolor de garganta
- Stomach pain / Dolor abdominal
- Muscle pain / Dolor muscular
- Joint pain / Dolor articular
- Chest pain / Dolor en el pecho
- Back pain / Dolor de espalda

### Fever & Temperature:
- Fever / Fiebre / Temperatura alta
- Chills / Escalofríos

### Respiratory:
- Cough / Tos
- Shortness of breath / Dificultad para respirar
- Nasal congestion / Congestión nasal
- Runny nose / Secreción nasal

### Digestive:
- Nausea / Náuseas
- Vomiting / Vómito
- Diarrhea / Diarrea
- Loss of appetite / Pérdida de apetito

### General:
- Fatigue / Fatiga / Cansancio
- Dizziness / Mareo
- Weakness / Debilidad
- Insomnia / Insomnio

## 🤖 How It Works:

1. **Type your symptoms** in the chat box (after completing consent form)
2. **AI extracts** relevant medical terms from your message
3. **API call** sends symptoms to medical analysis service
4. **Chart displays** top 5 most probable diagnoses with percentages
5. **Results show** what symptoms were analyzed

## ⚠️ Important Notes:

- This is for **educational purposes only**
- **Not a substitute** for professional medical advice
- For **emergencies**, contact medical services immediately
- System recognizes symptoms in **Spanish and English**
- If no specific symptoms detected, entire message is analyzed
